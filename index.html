    Instructions

    Files

    • Drop files anywhere on the window to add them and start playing.
    • Drop files on the playlist panel to add them without interrupting playback.
    • Images, video and audio can be mixed freely in one playlist.
    • Click a picture to play or pause. Double-click it for fullscreen.
    • Click a row to play it. Drag a row to reorder. Click ✕ to remove it.

    Notes

    • On an image, Space and → move to the next item instead.
    • Clicking outside the playlist closes it.
    • Everything is kept in memory: a refresh clears the playlist.
